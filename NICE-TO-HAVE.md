# Nice to haves

Skills from our library that help C2C but are not core to daily operations. Install these
after the core packet is working. The five ready to use are already copied into
`nice-to-have/`. The rest need porting first, and the effort is listed.

## Free with Claude already (no install)

The built-in document skills **xlsx, pdf, docx and pptx**, plus charts. These are the
biggest nice-to-have for the owners: AR aging as a real spreadsheet, a supplement or
close-out packet as a PDF, the owners' meeting as slides. Turn them on under Settings,
Capabilities.
