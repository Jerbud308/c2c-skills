# Job meeting prep

Read the `c2c-context` skill first.

## Gather

- The job or prospect record: address, scope, status, estimate, what has been billed and
  paid, open supplements, photos (CompanyCam), permits.
- The email and text thread with this person or company: last three exchanges, anything
  promised by either side, anything unanswered.
- For a new commercial prospect or property manager: their properties, roof types if known,
  public reviews of their buildings, who else they use. Cite every outside fact; if it
  cannot be verified, leave it out.

## Shape by meeting type

- **Adjuster / reinspection:** claim number, carrier, what the carrier approved vs. our
  estimate, line items in dispute with the photo or measurement that supports each, and
  the documents to bring.
- **Property manager / commercial pitch:** their portfolio, the maintenance or service
  contract offer, one past job most like theirs, the question that qualifies them.
- **Homeowner:** what they were told last, what they are worried about, the money picture
  (deductible, what insurance covers, what they owe), next step to ask for.
- **Sub / vendor:** open invoices, quality or schedule issues, what we need from them.

## Output (one phone screen)

```
<meeting> - <when> - <where>
WHO: <name, role, history with C2C in one line>
WHERE THINGS STAND: <3 lines, numbers cited>
OPEN PROMISES: <ours> / <theirs>
GET OUT OF THIS MEETING:
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...
BRING: <documents / photos>
```

## Rules

- Facts only from records or cited sources. Unknowns go under "ask in the meeting".
- After the meeting, offer to run `meeting-to-tasks` on the notes.
